GreenEye is an initiative designed to bridge the knowledge gap for laypeople regarding local vegetation. By leveraging the power of Computer Vision on mobile devices, users can instantly identify plants like pineapple (nanas), banana (pisang), and other Indonesian varieties simply by pointing their camera.

## 🛠️ My Role: AI Engineer

In this project, I was responsible for the end-to-end AI development lifecycle, focusing on making deep learning accessible on low-power devices:

- **Model Fine-Tuning:** I leveraged the **MobileNetV2** architecture—selected for its optimal balance between accuracy and resource efficiency—and performed fine-tuning on a curated dataset of Indonesian plants.
- **Edge Optimization:** To ensure the app could run smoothly on standard smartphones, me and my team converted the trained model into **Keras** format, optimizing it for real-time mobile inference.
- **Data Pipeline:** Curated and pre-processed plant imagery to ensure robust detection under various lighting conditions common in outdoor environments.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Real-time Identification:** Instant plant classification using the smartphone's camera feed.
- **Plant Encyclopedia:** Provides detailed information about identified species, helping users learn about local biodiversity.
- **User Experience:** Includes features to save favorite plants and share identification results with others.

## 🎯 Technical Takeaway

This project deepened my understanding of **Edge AI**. It taught me that building a great model is only half the battle; the real challenge lies in optimizing that model to perform efficiently "at the edge" without relying on heavy cloud compute resources.
